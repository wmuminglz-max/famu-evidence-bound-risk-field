## Interpreting fingerprints

`MANIFEST_SHA256.json` verifies files in the current directory. `manifests/source_fingerprints.json` links each controlled original's `original_sha256` to the repository file's `released_sha256`. Originals are described by logical identifiers. The controlled inventory's `verification` field distinguishes fingerprints recomputed from local files from those recorded in historical inventories.

Code, input, and weight hashes in experimental logs identify the objects used at the time. De-identified input hashes are consistent with references in scoring keys and archived predictions, allowing internal correspondence to be checked. Archived predictions originate from the original experiments; generating predictions on de-identified text constitutes a new experiment.

## Reproduction scope

The repository provides code, input templates, data splits, condition and evidence identifiers, training protocols, de-identified expert answers, and multi-seed archived predictions. Included predictions and scoring keys support score recomputation. Exact reruns of original training and inference also require controlled original weights, feature caches, unchanged inputs, and the corresponding environment.

See [R11 results](../experiments/student/R11_results.en.md) for student-model contiguous-span and replay experiments.
